GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
first rep.

A few things to know about LiftRank.

Can I download LiftRank today?+

LiftRank is an iOS app in development, for iOS 17 and later. There isn’t a public App Store or TestFlight download linked here yet. You can explore the preview above or visit the GitHub project for setup instructions.

Is this the real app?+

This website is a guided preview, built around the current app. Workouts, ranks, people, and trainer messages here are examples. Nothing is saved to an account, and refreshing the page resets the demo.

How do strength ranks work?+

The app estimates strength from recorded sets, then uses bodyweight and an exercise-specific scoring curve to produce scores from 1 to 1,000. Nine tiers run from Wood to Olympian. You can see ranks for exercises, muscle groups, and overall strength.

Does LiftRank need an internet connection?+

Core workout records and strength ranks are stored locally. Live community features and AI tools need a connection and their configured services. The website demo uses only sample data in your browser.

What about Apple Health and Apple Watch?+

The app includes Apple Health support and a Watch companion. Available tracking depends on your device, OS version, build configuration, and permissions. Real Watch sensor tracking still needs device validation as development continues.
